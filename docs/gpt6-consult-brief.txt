4. OUR IDEA MENU (v0 draft — critique this)
-------------------------------------------
A) Demand Index (core, rule-based):
   DI(D) = w1*official_holiday(D) + w2*weekend(D) + w3*seasonality(month of D,
   from review curve) + w4*rival_signal(D). Map 0-100 to classes
   normal/high/peak/super-peak. Price(D) = base_price * multiplier(DI),
   clamped by floor/ceiling derived from rival price distribution.

B) Booking-curve / pace layer (from first_seen):
   For each future night D and lead L = D - today, compare rivals' current
   booked-share with the booked-share observed at the same lead in past
   snapshots -> pace ratio. Positive pace = hot night early -> raise price
   sooner. Cold-start problem: only 29 days of history. Plan: pool all 34
   rivals (34x data) + empirical-Bayes shrinkage toward the average curve.

C) Year-view seasonality from 7,646 reviews + 1405/1406 holiday overlay.

D) Rival price-signal: rivals raising price or minimum-stay for night D is
   a demand proxy; price changes are already tracked in history DB.

E) Pro rulebook (copied from commercial tools):
   * Lead-time ladder: still-free nights get discounts at L=7/3/1 days;
     hot far-out nights get a premium.
   * Dynamic minimum stay: peak holiday weekends -> 2-3 night minimum.
   * Orphan-night rule: single free night sandwiched between bookings ->
     special fill-it price.
   * Absolute floor (cost-based) and ceiling (highest comparable rival).

F) Forecast-vs-actual tracker: every night, log what v1 predicted 30/14/7
   days earlier for the nights that just elapsed vs what actually happened
   (own occupancy + rivals). Show MAE honestly on the dashboard.

G) (Later, >=6 months data) gradient-boosting / regression on the features
   above. Explicitly out of scope for v1.
